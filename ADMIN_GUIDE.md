# Passbook: guide for administrators

## Who can do what
| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Create expenses, submit them, fix and resubmit rejected ones, see only their own |
| **Manager** | Approve or reject expenses from their team; see their team's spending dashboard |
| **Admin** | Everything a manager can across the whole company, plus mark approved expenses as paid and manage people |

Managers and admins do not file expenses. If someone needs to both file and approve,
give them two accounts.

## Teams
- Assign each employee to a manager (People -> the "Reports to" menu).
- A manager sees and approves **only their own team**.
- Employees with **no manager** can be reviewed by **any** manager, so nothing gets stuck.
- Nobody can approve their own expense. Drafts are private until the employee submits.
- If you demote or disable a manager, their team goes back to "no manager".

## Everyday tasks (People page)
- **Add a person:** "Add person", fill in details, copy the temporary password and give it to them
  privately. They must choose their own password at first sign-in.
- **Forgot password:** "Reset password" next to their name, and give them the new temporary password.
- **Someone leaves:** "Disable". They can no longer sign in; their past expenses stay on record.
- **Change a role or team:** use the menus on their row.

## Paying expenses
Payments page: approved expenses waiting to be paid. After you pay the person outside the system,
press "Mark reimbursed".

## Dashboard
Managers see their team's spending; admins see the whole company: totals, monthly trend,
categories, approval pipeline, top spenders, policy flags (expenses over category limits).
